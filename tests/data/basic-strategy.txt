Basic strategy for the table on the profile: six decks, dealer stands on soft
17, double after split, late surrender, blackjack pays 3:2.

This file is not used to play. Nothing in scripts/ reads a chart — every action
is priced from the cards left in the shoe. It is here as a check on that: the
calculator has to reproduce all 260 cells below from a full shoe, and if it ever
stops doing so, either it is wrong or a rule changed underneath it.

Columns are the dealer upcard.

  H   hit
  S   stand
  D   double, or hit where doubling is not allowed
  Ds  double, or stand where doubling is not allowed
  P   split
  R   surrender, or hit where surrender is not allowed

Hard totals are built without a ten wherever they can be, so hard 16 is 9+7 and
not 10+6. Two hands with the same total and different cards are not the same
hand, and the calculator will happily tell you so.
